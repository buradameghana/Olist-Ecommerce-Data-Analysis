# Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis

An interactive Business Intelligence project analysing the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset using **Microsoft Power BI**.

## Project Overview

This project explores e-commerce data to understand sales performance, delivery operations, product pricing and customer behaviour. It transforms raw data into interactive dashboards and meaningful business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query – Data preparation and transformation
- DAX – Measures and calculations

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Delivery Performance
- Total and delivered orders
- Cancellation and delivery rates
- Late and on-time deliveries
- Order status distribution

### 2. Revenue Analysis
- Product revenue and freight
- Average product price
- Top-performing sellers
- Revenue by product category

### 3. Product Pricing Analysis
- Average selling price by category
- Highest and lowest selling prices
- Unique products and products sold

### 4. Customer Analysis
- Unique customer distribution
- Repeat customer analysis
- State-wise customer insights

## Key Business Insights

- São Paulo (SP) has the largest share of customers.
- Beauty & Health generated the highest category revenue in the analysis.
- Bed, Table & Bath had the highest number of items sold.
- Product categories showed considerable differences in average selling prices.
- Delivery performance and repeat-customer patterns were explored through interactive dashboards.

## Project Outcome

Developed a four-page interactive Power BI dashboard to explore e-commerce performance and support data-driven business understanding.
